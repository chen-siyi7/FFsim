# ffsim 0.1.0

* First release.
* Core FF transform `g_eta()` and CRT sampler `rcrt()`.
* Conditional weight-layer experiment `ff_weight_layer()`.
* Full finite weak-limit HDP Gibbs sampler `ff_gibbs()` and clustering sweep
  `ff_cluster()`.
* Top-level driver `ff_run()` writing CSV tables and PDF figures.
